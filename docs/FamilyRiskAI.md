**FamilyRiskAI** is the AI chatbox of FamilyRisk, built for the clinical geneticists, genetic counselors and researchers who use the web server. Through natural-language conversation, it helps users explore the screening knowledge base, build customized screening lists, configure analyses, and quickly understand the results of finished analyses.FamilyRiskAI does not provide a diagnosis, prognosis or treatment recommendation. All screening findings need confirmation and interpretation by a genetic counselor or clinical geneticist.

FamilyRiskAI can be opened from the purple button (✦) in the bottom-right corner of the [homepage](Homepage.md).


### Functionality

#### 1. Explore the screening knowledge base
FamilyRiskAI finds genes, diseases and preset panels in the curated screening list. It searches the gene–disease associations, each with its inheritance mode, disorder group and GenCC validity, and it describes and compares the 18 preset panels, including their contents and overlap.

*Example questions:*

- "Which preset panels include *CFTR*?"
- "What is the inheritance mode and GenCC validity of *IDH3A*?"
- "How do the BabySeq and EarlyCheck panels differ?"

#### 2. Build customized screening lists
FamilyRiskAI assembles custom gene panels from the curated list. Only genes from the curated list are accepted: a gene outside the list is reported as such rather than added. The proposed panel arrives as a card that can be applied to the analysis form, or downloaded as a gene list, with one click.

*Example question:*

- "Build a panel of autosomal recessive metabolic disorders with definitive GenCC validity."

#### 3. Configure analyses and filtering options
FamilyRiskAI explains each analysis and drafts its configuration, including the optional filtering parameters. It reads each analysis's live parameters with their defaults and allowed values, and checks every suggested configuration against them. The proposed configuration arrives as a card that the user can apply to the analysis form with one click.

*Example questions:*

- "What parameters can I set for family-level carrier screening, and what are their defaults?"
- "Set up a newborn screening analysis using the ACMG secondary findings panel."

#### 4. Pinpoint, summarise and explain variants
FamilyRiskAI reads the results of your own finished analyses and of the six public demo analyses. It helps you quickly pinpoint variants of interest, and summarises and explains the findings.

*Example questions:*

- "Summarise the screen-positive findings of my newborn screening demo."
- "Which variants in this family analysis are in autosomal recessive genes?"
